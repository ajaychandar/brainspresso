Brainspresso Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Brainspresso ("the app") is an alarm clock app created by Ajay Chandar ("the developer").

Summary

Brainspresso does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal data. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no accounts, and makes no network connections of its own.

Data stored on your device

Your alarms (time, label, repeat days, chosen sound, vibration setting) and your puzzle settings are saved only in private storage on your own device. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions and why the app asks for them

  • Exact alarms (USE_EXACT_ALARM / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): to ring at the exact time you set.
  • Notifications: to show the ringing-alarm notification.
  • Full-screen notifications and foreground service: to show the puzzle screen over the lock screen and keep the alarm sound playing until you solve it.
  • Wake lock and vibrate: to wake the screen and vibrate when an alarm rings.
  • Receive boot completed: to re-arm your alarms after the device restarts.
  • Physical activity (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION), optional: used only if you turn on "Walk to stop the alarm". The app reads your phone's step-detector sensor on the device to count steps while an alarm is ringing. Step data is not saved, not transmitted, and not used for anything else.

Sounds

Alarm sounds are chosen from the ringtones already on your device through Android's system picker. The app does not access your media library beyond the sound you select.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
